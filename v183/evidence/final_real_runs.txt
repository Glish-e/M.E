## Latent phase-5 defects FOUND and fixed by this phase's red runs
- P6-D1: the offline guard patched earnings _px_fetch — the REAL engine's
  price fetcher is _pfetch (setting _px_fetch was a silent no-op; offline
  prices could still hit the network). Both names now guarded; caught by
  the online-side AttributeError in the red run.
- P6-D2: meds has NO transport seam and calls yfinance directly — in
  offline mode with yfinance installed, meds' price inputs opened sockets
  (invisible to phase-5 tests because the suite blocks sockets, turning
  them into engine-internal errors only). Offline now makes yfinance look
  ABSENT (sys.modules valve, restored after the run) — verified: 0.2s
  snapshot regime, zero sockets.
- P6-D3: asyncio's event loop opens an AF_UNIX socketpair; a socket gate
  must block only AF_INET/AF_INET6 (the suite always did; documented here
  so nobody "fixes" it into flakiness).
